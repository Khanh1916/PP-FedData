## Item 5: IDS trained on synthetic data only (TSTR), IDS-side options

`base` = 5,000 synthetic rows per class (as in the study); `all rows` = 20,000 per class; `+ weights` = class-probability weights chosen on validation (mean of 3 seeds). Validation and test are class-balanced like the synthetic data, so the weights do not correct a class prior: they mostly lower NORMAL (and BCF for most FedDP-Marginal sets), which an IDS trained on synthetic rows predicts too often. An improvement of the IDS, not of the generator; on traffic with another class mix the weights must be chosen again.
